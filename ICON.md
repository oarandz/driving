## Current generation prompt

Create a flat minimalist app icon from scratch: a single large solid blue uppercase letter L, colour #125BDD, optically centred on a completely solid pure white #FFFFFF square canvas. The L is bold geometric sans-serif, sharp right angles, about 60% of the square height. No border. Use only two perfectly uniform solid colours: white and blue, plus antialiasing at the edges. Absolutely no bevel, embossing, lighting, shading, texture, grain, gradients, gloss, shadows, 3D effects or decorative elements. The entire background must be OPAQUE WHITE, never transparent or black. Clean modern flat vector-style appearance, front facing, square 1024x1024. Only the letter L. This is the finished image asset for an iPhone Home Screen icon, not a mockup.
